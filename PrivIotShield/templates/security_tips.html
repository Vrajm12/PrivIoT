Network Security
  • Enable Network Segmentation: Create a separate network (VLAN) for IoT devices to isolate them from your main network. This limits potential damage if a device is compromised.
  • Use Strong Wi-Fi Encryption: Enable WPA3 encryption on your router. If WPA3 isn't available, use WPA2 at minimum. Never use WEP encryption.
  • Disable UPnP: Universal Plug and Play can be exploited by attackers. Disable it on your router unless absolutely necessary.
  • Enable Firewall: Use your router's built-in firewall and consider implementing additional firewall rules for IoT devices.
Access Control
  • Change Default Passwords: Immediately change default usernames and passwords on all IoT devices. Use unique, strong passwords for each device.
  • Enable Two-Factor Authentication: If available, enable 2FA on device accounts and associated mobile apps.
  • Limit Remote Access: Only enable remote access when necessary. Use VPN instead of exposing devices directly to the internet.
  • Review Permissions: Regularly audit which apps and services have access to your devices and revoke unnecessary permissions.
Updates & Maintenance
  • Enable Automatic Updates: Turn on automatic firmware updates when available to ensure devices receive security patches promptly.
  • Check for Updates Regularly: Many devices don't support automatic updates. Check manufacturer websites monthly for firmware updates.
  • Retire Old Devices: Replace devices that no longer receive security updates. Unsupported devices are security risks.
  • Regular Security Scans: Use PrivIoT to scan your devices weekly and address vulnerabilities promptly.
Privacy Protection
  • Review Privacy Settings: Check what data your devices collect and configure privacy settings to minimize data collection.
  • Disable Unnecessary Features: Turn off microphones, cameras, and other sensors when not in use.
  • Read Privacy Policies: Understand what data manufacturers collect and how it's used before purchasing devices.
  • Local Processing: Prefer devices that process data locally rather than sending it to cloud services.
